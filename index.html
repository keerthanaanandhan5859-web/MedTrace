One operational platform

The software follows the technical approach in the SIH proposal: AMR hardware, backend services and a web control layer.

01 · AMR & sensing

Battery-electric movement, LiDAR, RGB/depth camera, wheel feedback, QR scanning, safety sensing and sealed compartments.

02 · Backend & data

Node.js, Express and MongoDB store waste records, telemetry, QR scans, alerts and chain-of-custody events.

03 · Dashboard & traceability

Hospital, admin and CBWTF workspaces provide monitoring, alerts, tracking and treatment records.

AI

AI-assisted classification

Waste category verification is structured for later YOLO/CNN integration; prototype mode is explicitly labelled.

QR

Digital identity

Each waste bag receives a unique Waste ID and QR code for traceability.

AMR

Fleet telemetry

Multiple hospital AMRs can connect independently and report their actual state.

AL

Operational alerts

Battery, compartment fill, obstacle, human-detection and emergency events are stored when reported.

GPS

Location events

GPS coordinates are recorded when the hospital or AMR supplies them.

LOG

Digital chain of custody

Collection, transit, receipt and treatment events remain linked to the waste record.

End-to-end workflow

Aligned to the proposed hospital-to-CBWTF process.

01Register bag

Hospital records source, category, weight, image and location.

02AI verify + QR

Category is checked and a unique digital identity is created.

03AMR collects

Robot telemetry and QR handover events are recorded.

04CBWTF receives

Transport, receipt and treatment status are logged.
